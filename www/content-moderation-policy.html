Content Moderation Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026 — Applies to the game ORBEX / POLARIX

ORBEX / POLARIX includes social features (lobby chat, room chat, direct messages, friends, and multiplayer rooms) where players can interact and send text messages to each other. This policy explains how we keep those interactions safe.

1. Community Rules

Players must not use the App to:

  • Harass, bully, threaten, or abuse other players
  • Send hateful, discriminatory, or sexually explicit content
  • Impersonate another person, or use an inappropriate/offensive username
  • Spam, advertise, or send scam/phishing links in chat
  • Cheat, exploit bugs, or use unauthorized third-party tools
  • Share another player's personal information without consent

2. How Users Can Report or Block

Every player profile, friend, and chat conversation has a built-in Report and Block option, accessible in a few taps:

  • Block: instantly stops the blocked player from messaging you or sending friend requests. You can unblock at any time from your Friends list.
  • Report: opens a reason picker (Cheating / Unfair Play, Harassment, Inappropriate Name, Spam, Other). Submitting a report sends it to our moderation queue for review.

3. How Reports Are Reviewed

Reports are reviewed manually by the developer/admin using a secure, authenticated admin tool that only admin accounts can access — regular players never have access to this data or to other players' data.

StepWhat happens
1Report is submitted with reporter ID, reported player ID, reason, timestamp, and the recent chat messages between the two players (for context).
2Report enters a moderation queue, visible only to authorized admin accounts.
3The admin reviews the report: the chat context, the reported player's in-game stats (coins, score, level), and their last-online activity, to judge whether the report reflects genuine unfair play, cheating, or abusive behavior — not just a personal disagreement between players.
4If confirmed, the admin takes action directly (warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban/account deletion). Admin access to player data and account controls exists solely for moderation and fair-play enforcement, and is not used to view or modify player data for any other purpose.

To prevent abuse of the report system, a player cannot submit repeated reports against the same player within a short cooldown window.

4. Enforcement Actions

Once a report is confirmed, the admin can act directly on the account:

  • Warning: for minor or first-time issues.
  • Temporary ban: the account is banned and shown an "Account Banned" notice on login; it can be unbanned by the admin if appropriate (e.g., appeal or mistaken report).
  • Permanent ban or account deletion: for serious or repeated violations, the admin can ban the account permanently or delete it entirely.

These moderation controls are used only in response to a genuine report or safety concern — never for any other purpose.

5. Response Time

We aim to review reports within a reasonable timeframe. Urgent safety issues (threats, explicit content, suspected minors at risk) are prioritized.

6. Protecting Minors

We do not knowingly allow adult content, grooming behavior, or solicitation of personal information from minors. Any such report is treated as a priority and can result in an immediate ban.
