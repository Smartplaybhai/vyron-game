Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026 — Applies to the game ORBEX / POLARIX

This Privacy Policy explains what information ORBEX / POLARIX ("the App") collects, why, and how you can control or delete it. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's unique ID and, where provided, your display name. If you play as a Guest, an anonymous unique ID is created instead — no personal information is required.
  • In-game profile data: your chosen in-game name/username, level, XP, coins, owned skins, match stats, and settings.
  • Social features: friends list, friend requests, and messages you send in lobby chat, room chat, or direct messages with friends.
  • Reports: if you report another player, we store the report reason and the accounts involved so it can be reviewed.
  • Advertising data: if the App shows ads (via Google AdMob), the ad provider may collect an advertising identifier and related data as described in Google's Ads Policy.
  • Technical data: basic connection/session data needed to run real-time multiplayer (e.g., online/offline status, room codes you join).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and restore your player profile and progress across sessions and devices.
  • To provide multiplayer matchmaking, friends, and chat features.
  • To keep the community safe (blocking, reporting, and moderation).
  • To show ads that help keep the game free (if applicable).

3. Data Storage

Game data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), which applies Google's own security and infrastructure protections. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country) without appropriate consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

5. Your Choices & Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data (profile, coins, skins, friends, chat history) at any time:

  • In the App: go to Settings → Delete Account.
  • Without the App: email us at the address below with your in-game Player ID and we will delete your data within a reasonable time.

You can also log out at any time from Settings → Log Out without deleting your data.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (authentication & database)
  • Google / Google Sign-In
  • Google AdMob (if ads are shown)
